zen-recorder: what the recording's building blocks cost in this browser

Measures the steps of the recorder's video pipeline on this machine: drawing a 1080p WebRTC video and a worker-painted canvas (Zoom) into a 1920x1080 canvas, turning the canvas into a VideoFrame, compositing with WebGL2, and encoding with WebCodecs (VP9, VP8, AV1, H.264; hardware and software). Takes two to three minutes. Keep this tab in front and do not use the machine meanwhile. When it is done, press Copy results and send them.
